Delicious Appetizers

Quick and easy recipes to start your meal right

1. Garlic Bread Bites

Ingredients:

  • 1 French baguette
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Slice the baguette into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Mix melted butter, garlic, and parsley together.
  4. Brush each bread piece with the mixture and top with Parmesan.
  5. Bake for 10–12 minutes or until golden and crispy.
  6. Serve warm and enjoy!

2. Caprese Skewers

Ingredients:

  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Fresh mozzarella balls (bocconcini)
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Balsamic glaze
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Skewers or toothpicks

Steps:

  1. On each skewer, thread a tomato, a basil leaf, and a mozzarella ball.
  2. Repeat to fill each skewer.
  3. Drizzle with balsamic glaze.
  4. Sprinkle with salt and pepper.
  5. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
